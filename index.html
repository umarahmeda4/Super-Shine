What We Offer

Expert Detailing for Every Vehicle

From a quick body wash to a full executive detail, our trained team restores your car to showroom condition.

🚿

Body Wash

Thorough exterior hand wash removing road grime, dust, and contaminants. Available for saloons and 4x4s.

From KSh 300 / saloon
⚙️

Engine Steam Wash

High-pressure steam cleans your engine bay safely, removing grease, oil, and road debris.

From KSh 1,500 / saloon

Executive Wash

Full interior & exterior detailing including vacuuming, leather care, dashboard shine, and exterior polish.

From KSh 2,000 / saloon simple

Machine Polish

From KSh 1,500 / vehicle

Under Wash Restoration

High-pressure underbody cleaning removing mud, salt, and corrosives that damage chassis components.

From KSh 3,000 / saloon

A/C refrigerant top-up to restore your vehicle's cooling to peak performance. Leak checks available.

From KSh 4,000 / saloon
